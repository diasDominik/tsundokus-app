Tsundoku keeps track of the manga you've ordered, from checkout until it's on your shelf, and of the pile you still have to read.

Pre-orders slip, parcels come from different shops, and late-night hauls are easy to forget. Tsundoku puts every order in one place, so you always know what's coming and what's late.

📦 Every order at a glance
Ordered, on the way, delayed or received: see each order's status, price, shop and expected date. Search, filter and sort your list in seconds.

📷 Scan instead of typing
Scan the barcode on the back cover and Tsundoku looks the book up by its ISBN. When the parcel arrives, scan it again to mark the order as received.

⏳ Delays under control
When a release slips, enter the new date and Tsundoku keeps a timeline of every change. Delivery estimates learn from how fast each shop has delivered to you before.

🔔 Reminders
Get a notification when an order is overdue or when a delayed order's new date comes around, at a time you choose.

📚 Series and reading pile
See which volumes of a series you have, which are on the way and which are missing. Mark volumes as want to read, reading or read, and watch your pile shrink.

📊 Stats
What you've spent, how long deliveries take, which shops are often late, and your favourite shops and publishers.

🏠 Home screen widgets
Your next arrivals and your reading pile, right on your home screen.

📶 Works offline
Your orders are stored on your device, so you can check them anywhere. Changes sync to your account as soon as you're back online.

🔒 Your data, your choice
Sign in with a passkey using your fingerprint, face or screen lock. Export all your orders as CSV or JSON at any time, and delete your account whenever you want.

🌍 In your language
Available in 22 languages.

Tsundoku (積ん読) is the Japanese word for buying books and letting them pile up unread. This app won't stop your pile from growing, but you'll always know what's in it.
