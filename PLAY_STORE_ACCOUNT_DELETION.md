# Account Deletion Request - Tsundoku

In accordance with Google Play's Data Safety requirements, this page provides instructions on how to request the deletion of your Tsundoku account and its associated data.

## How to request deletion

You can request account deletion either directly through the Tsundoku app or by contacting us via email.

### Option 1: In-App Deletion (Recommended)

To delete your account from within the app:
1. Open the **Tsundoku** app.
2. Go to the **Settings** screen (accessible from the main navigation).
3. Select **Delete account**.
4. You will see a confirmation screen. To prevent accidental deletions, you must type **DELETE** in the confirmation field.
5. Tap the **Delete account** button to finalize the process.

Your account and all associated data (including reading lists and order history) will be deleted immediately.

### Option 2: Request via Email

If you no longer have the app installed or cannot access your account, you can request deletion by email:
1. Send an email to **0tuaf66s@anonaddy.me**.
2. Use the subject line **"Account Deletion Request"**.
3. Include the **email address** or **username** associated with your account so we can identify you.
4. We will confirm your identity by replying to your registered email address.
5. Once confirmed, your account and data will be deleted within **30 days**.

## Data that is deleted

When you delete your account, we permanently remove:
- **Account credentials:** Your email address, username, and authentication tokens.
- **Personal data:** Your manga reading lists, order tracking data, and app preferences.

## Data that may be retained

- **Backups:** Data may persist in our encrypted system backups for up to **90 days** before being completely overwritten.
- **Legal Requirements:** We may retain certain records for a longer period if required by law (e.g., for financial or regulatory compliance).

## Locally stored data

Uninstalling the app will remove all locally cached data from your device. However, this does not delete your account from our servers. To delete your account permanently, please follow the "How to request deletion" steps above.
