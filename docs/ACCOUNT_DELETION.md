# Tsundoku — Account & Data Deletion

**Android App:** Tsundoku (`uk.tsundokus`)

This page explains how to request deletion of your Tsundoku account and the data
associated with it.

## How to request deletion

1. Send an email to **0tuaf66s@anonaddy.me**.
2. Use the subject line **"Delete my account"**.
3. Include the **email address** you registered with, so we can locate your
   account.
4. We confirm the request by replying to your registered email address.
5. Your account and data are deleted within **30 days** of a confirmed request.

## What data is deleted

When your account is deleted, we remove:

- Account credentials: your email address, username, and password.
- Your manga orders and reading-list entries.

## What data may be retained, and for how long

- Encrypted backups are purged within **90 days**.
- Where we are legally required to keep certain records, we retain them for the
  legally mandated period and delete them afterwards.

## Data stored only on your device

Uninstalling the Tsundoku app removes all locally cached data (orders and login
tokens) from that device.
